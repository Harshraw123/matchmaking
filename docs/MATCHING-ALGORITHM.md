# The Date Crew (TDC) — Matrimonial Matching Engine Architecture

### Paragraph 1 — Algorithm Design & Scoring Model
The Date Crew matching engine is an explainable, deterministic multi-stage evaluation pipeline that operates independently of stochastic language models. In the initial stage, candidate profiles undergo strict eligibility filtering to exclude identical individuals, same-gender pairings (under the assessment's opposite-gender mandate), candidates with existing active introductions, and candidates who violate explicit non-negotiable dealbreakers—such as irreconcilable stances on children or inter-city relocation. Eligible candidates are then scored across seven normalized, configurable dimensions: mutual partner-preference alignment (30%), children & long-term values (20%), lifestyle & interests (15%), career & education compatibility (15%), relocation & location (10%), language compatibility (5%), and profile completeness & verification (5%). To reflect distinct matchmaking requirements, the engine executes gender-specific evaluation heuristics: male customer evaluations accommodate stated age brackets, height differentials, and financial flexibility, whereas female customer evaluations prioritize profession alignment, education pedigree, and career stability. In all cases, two-way reciprocal compatibility is computed so that candidate preferences are simultaneously checked against the client. Any absent attributes are transparently treated as unknown rather than penalized as failures, and an independent score coverage metric is computed alongside the overall score so that matchmakers can distinguish between well-evidenced high scores and sparsely supported ratings.

### Paragraph 2 — AI Integration & Grounded Synthesis
Artificial intelligence is integrated strictly as an augmentation layer via a server-side OpenAI integration, ensuring that core compatibility scores, eligibility status, and ranking orders remain 100% deterministic and tamper-proof. When match recommendations are prepared, the system feeds the LLM only verified profile facts, calculated factor scores, confirmed strengths, identified friction points, and missing data points, governed by strict prompt constraints forbidding the invention of unstated biographical details. All LLM responses are validated at runtime using a strict Zod schema to produce structured matchmaker rationales and personalized introductory draft notes. If the OpenAI API key is missing, rate-limited, times out, or returns malformed JSON, the application immediately and silently engages an internal deterministic rule-based template engine that generates professional explanations and introduction drafts without disrupting the matchmaker's workflow or altering ranking calculations.

### Paragraph 3 — Assumptions, Fairness, and Ethical Limitations
The numerical scores produced by this engine are operational decision-support heuristics designed to assist human matchmakers in prioritizing review queues; they are not scientifically proven predictors of lifelong relationship happiness or romantic connection. Sensitive cultural attributes—specifically caste and religion—are optional, access-controlled fields that are strictly isolated from default scoring algorithms and never inferred through proxy attributes like family names or geography. Furthermore, the factor weight distribution represents a product-level baseline for high-intent urban professionals and should be continuously refined through empirical match outcome feedback, bias audits, and client consent frameworks before deployment in high-stakes matrimonial operations.
